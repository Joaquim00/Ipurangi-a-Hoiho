Traditionally Cantonese people have had very low interests in (systemic) Cantonese Romanisation on average.\\
\bigskip

\smallskip
“Nei ting yat faan ng faan hall ah bb?”\\

“Farn ah. Hui yuen colleague uk kei then farn lai.”\\
\quad (Yes, I will come back after visiting my colleagues' home.)\\
“Nei gei dim farn lai ah?”\\
\quad (What time would you come back?)\\
“8-9 dim la I think.”\\
\quad (8 to 9 o'clock I think.)\\
