\begin{frame}[fragile]{Yue–Pinghua \scriptsize{LAC 2012}}

{\small
Language Atlas of China (2\textsuperscript{nd} ed.) B1-18 粤语 (by 伍巍)\\
\bigskip
粤语内部有几个相对统一的特点：\\
\medskip
(1) 古全浊声母在多数方言中清化。清化后，有的读送气音，有的读不送气音，也有的平声、上声送气，去声、入声不送气。\\
{\scriptsize[MC voiced obstruent initials have become voiceless in \underline{most} dialects. After becoming voiceless, in some dialects they are aspirated, in some dialects they are unaspirated, in some dialects they are aspirated in tones AB and unaspirated in tones CD.]}\\
\medskip
(2) 古微母字今读双唇音，如“舞”、“文”、“万”、“网”等字今声母一律读[m-]，舞=母、尾=美、文=门、万=慢。\\
{\scriptsize[MC 微 [mw-/ɱ-] initial characters are pronounced bilabial in the modern language, e.g. 舞文万 all pronounced [m-] nowadays, e.g. [Cantonese] 舞 mou5 = 母 mou5, 尾 mei5 = 美 mei5, 文 man4 = 門 mun4, 萬 maan6 = 慢 maan6}
}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{Yue–Pinghua \scriptsize{LAC 2012}}

{\small
(3) 齿音声母一般只有一套，多数方言读舌叶音[tʃ- tʃ'- ʃ-]。\\
{\scriptsize[There is usually only one set of sibilants, in most dialects they are laminal [tʃ- tʃʰ- ʃ-].]\\
\smallskip
\textcolor{red}{[Actually not uncommon to have 2 series, especially e.g. /ʃ/ vs. /ɬ-/]}
}\\
\medskip
(4) 见组及晓母字不论洪细音，声母多不腭化，今读舌根音[k- k'- ŋ- h-]，娇≠焦、溪≠妻、件≠践、惊≠精、脚≠雀、晓≠小。\\
{\scriptsize[MC 見 [k-] series and 曉 [h-] initial characters, no matter whether it is followed by 洪 [open vowels / u] or 細 [i / y], the initial is usually not 腭化 palatalised, they are nowadays pronounced velar [k- kʰ- ŋ- h-], [Cantonese] 嬌 giu1 ≠ 焦 ziu1, 溪 kai1 ≠ 妻 cai1, 件 gin6 ≠ 踐 cin5, 驚 ging1 ≠ 精 zing1, 腳 goek3 ≠ 雀 zoek3, 曉 hiu2 ≠ 小 siu2]}\\
\smallskip
部分方言流、深、臻三摄牙音细音字声母有齿化现象。
{\scriptsize[In some dialects, there is dentalisation with MC 流 [-iw], 深 [-im], 臻 [-in] groups of finals in front of 細音 [i / y]}\\
e.g. 玉林\ 九 tʃau˧ 球 tʃau˩˧˩ 金 tʃam˥˦ {\scriptsize[Canto. 九 kɐu˧˥ 球 kʰɐu˩ 金 kɐm˥]}
}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{Yue–Pinghua \scriptsize{LAC 2012}}

{\small
(5) 古溪母开口常用字多有读清喉擦音[h-]的现象；古溪母合口字（多与古晓母合口字合流）今多读[f-]，如广州话：苦=虎=府[fu˧˥]，宽=欢[fun˥]。\\
{\scriptsize[With 常用字 common characters, MC 開口 non-rounded 溪[kʰ-] initial often has the phenomenon of being pronounced as 清喉擦 voiceless glottal fricative [h-]; MC 合口 rounded 溪[kʰ-] initial is (mostly merged with MC 合口 rounded 曉 [h-] initial) now often pronounced [f-], e.g. Canton dialect 苦=虎=府[fu˧˥]，寬=歡[fun˥].]}\\
\medskip
{[Canto. 開 hoi1 口 hau2 坑 haang1]}\\
}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{Yue–Pinghua \scriptsize{LAC 2012}}

{\small
(6) 除邕浔粤语外，大多数方言无舌尖元音韵母。绝大多数粤语止摄开口齿音字韵母为舌面元音[-i]，部分粤语止摄开口精、庄组字韵母为[-y]或[-u]。\\
{\scriptsize[Except 邕潯 Yōngxún Yue / Jung1 Cam4 Jyut6, most dialects do not have 韻母 finals with 舌尖元音 apical vowels. In the vast majority of Yue dialects, MC 開口 non-rounded 止 [i] group of finals with 齒音 coronal initial is pronounced with 舌面元音 \enquote{normal} vowel [-i], in some Yue dialects MC 開口 non-rounded 止 [i] group of finals with 精 [ts-] 莊 [ʈʂ-] series of initials is pronounced as [-y] or [-u].]}\\
\medskip
[資\ 廣州 Canto. tsi˥ 桂平 Canto. tsz̩˥ 百色 Canto. ɬɮ̍˥ 順德大良 tsy˥˧]\\
\medskip
(7) 长短元音[a]与[ɐ(e)]基本成系统对立，如广州话：街 kai˥≠鸡 kɐi˥，三 ʃam˥≠心 ʃɐm˥，蛮 man˨˩≠民 mɐn˨˩，彭 p'aŋ˨˩≠凭 p'ɐŋ˨˩。在音质上，[a、ɐ]不但有时值长短的区别，也同时有舌位高低的区别。\\
{\scriptsize[Long and short vowels [a] and [ɐ(e)] basically form a systematic contrast, for example Cantonese 街 kai˥≠鸡 kɐi˥，三 ʃam˥≠心 ʃɐm˥，蛮 man˨˩≠民 mɐn˨˩，彭 p'aŋ˨˩≠凭 p'ɐŋ˨˩. In terms of quality, [a, ɐ] not only have a temporal length difference, there is also a difference in tongue height]}
}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{Yue–Pinghua \scriptsize{LAC 2012}}

{\small
(8) 流摄、臻摄开口一等与三等字不分洪细音，多数方言同韵。\\
{\scriptsize[MC 流 -iw and 臻 -in groups of finals, first (uw ən) and third division (juw jiw (j)in jɨn) characters, no matter whether it is followed by 洪 [open / u] or 細 [i/y] vowels, in most dialects they are the same final]}\\
\smallskip
[Canto. 樓=流 lau4, 走=酒 zau2, 狗=九 gau2, 根=斤 gan1]\\
\medskip
(9) 古阳声韵和入声韵今分别读[-m、-n、-ŋ]和[-p、-t、-k]两套阳、入配的韵尾。\\
{\scriptsize[MC 陽聲韻 nasal-ending finals and 入聲韻 plosive-ending finals are now read with [-m -n -ŋ] [-p -t -k] two sets of nasal and plosive codas]}\\
\smallskip
{[Canto. 針 zam1 文 man4 江 gong 1 汁 zap1 物 mat6 覺 gok3]}\\
}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{Yue–Pinghua \scriptsize{LAC 2012}}

{\small
(10) 平、上、去、入四调均分阴阳，入声调中阴入上下两分，有的阳入也上下两分，所以入声调通常有3个或4个。\\
{\scriptsize[Tones ABCD all split into upper and lower, tone D1 is also split into two, in some dialects tone D2 is also split into two, so there are usually 3 or 4 tone D's.]}\\
{[Canto. tone DS1 必 bit1 (*bat1), tone DL1 鱉 bit3, tone D2 別 bit6]}\\
\medskip
(11) 常用词汇中，家多称“屋企”，东西多称“嘢”，公鸡、母鸡说成“鸡公”、“鸡乸”，什么说成“乜”，视觉动词为“睇”，宰杀称“劏”，判断动词多为“系”。\\
{\scriptsize[Amongst common vocabulary, \enquote{home} is 屋企 uk1 kei2, \enquote{thing} is 嘢 je5, rooster and hen are 雞公 gai1 gung1, 雞乸 gai1 naa2, \enquote{what} is 乜 mat1, visual verb is 睇 tai2, \enquote{to butcher} is 劏 tong1, copula is usually 係 hai6.]}\\
}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{Yue–Pinghua \scriptsize{LAC 2012}}

{\small
(12) 量词独用具有指示功能，如“件衫好靓”（这件衣服很漂亮）、“部车坏左”（这辆车坏了）、“啲人走晒”（这些人跑光了），其中“件”、“部”、“啲”分别相当于北京话“这件”、“这辆”、“这些”。表示动作行为或现象持续的助词用“紧”，如“讲紧”（正在说话），“落紧雨”（正下着雨）；表示动作行为回复的助词一般都用“翻”，如“找翻两文我”（找回我两块钱）、“做翻先前[啲嘢]”（重新做起以前的那些工作）。\\
{\scriptsize[A classifier on its own has deitic function, e.g. 件衫好靚 gin6 saam1 hou2 leng3 (\textsc{clf} shirt) very beautiful) \enquote*{The shirt is beautiful}, 部車壞咗 bou6 ce1 waai6 zo2 (\textsc{clf} vehicle bad \textsc{pfv}) \enquote*{The car broke down}, 啲人走晒 di1 jan4 zau2 saai3 (\textsc{clf:mass} person leave \textsc{compl}) \enquote*{All the people left}. 件\ 部\ 啲 is equivalent to Beijing dialect 這件\ 這輛\ 這些 respectively. The progression of an action or state is expressed by the particle 緊 gan2, e.g. 講緊 gong2 gan2 (speak \textsc{prog}) \enquote{X is speaking}, 落緊雨 lok6 gan2 jyu5 (descend \textsc{prog} rain) \enquote*{it is raining}; returning action is expressed by the particle 翻 faan1, e.g. 找翻兩文我 zaau2 faan1 loeng5 man4*1 ngo5 (give.change \textsc{return} two \$ I) \enquote{give me change of \$/¥2 back}, 做翻先前啲嘢 zou6 faan1 sin1cin4 di1 je5 (do \textsc{return} before \textsc{clf:mass} thing \enquote*{Doing things [which I did] before again}.}\\
}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{Yue–Pinghua \scriptsize{Chan 2025}}

{\small 
1)內轉一等韻三等韻主元音央低化(至少不在i u的位置上)：\\
此點指九狗巾根金亙吉乞急刻等字的主元音不高於ǝ。[...]\\

\smallskip
\textcolor{red}{Basically: i/u splitting \& lowering, e.g. -iC > -jəC (> -əC)}\\
\begin{table}
    \flushleft
    \begin{tabular}{c|c c c c c}
         & 狗 & 九 & 陳 & 十 & 金\\
