\begin{frame}[fragile]{Phonotactics}

The changes from C(G)GVX to \textbf{CVX}:\\
Merger of GV, e.g.\\
\quad 香 *xjaŋ\textsuperscript{A} > Canto. (*hiɔŋ >) /hœŋ˥/\\ 
\quad \quad (cf. HK Hakka /hioŋ˩˧/)\\
\smallskip
Deletion of G, e.g.\\
\quad 菊 *kjuʷk > Canto. /kuk˥/\\ 
\quad \quad (cf. HK Hakka /kʰiuk˧/)\\
\smallskip
Deletion of V, e.g.\\
\quad 偏 *pʰjen\textsuperscript{A} > Canto. /pʰin˥/\\ 
\quad \quad (cf. HK Hakka /pʰien˩˧/)\\
\quad 勸 *kʰwjən\textsuperscript{C} > Canto. /hyn˧/\\
\quad \quad (cf. Nanning Ping. /kʷʰin˨˥/ {\scriptsize V also deleted})\\
\quad \quad (cf. Viet. /xwiə̯n˦\~{}˦˥/ {\scriptsize but /i/ is nucleus})\\
\end{frame}

    \begin{column}{0.5\textwidth} 
        \begin{itemize}
            \item zero onset is represented by zero, e.g. 屋 \emph{uk1} \enquote*{house}
            \item \emph{b- p-}, \emph{d- t-} etc. are voiceless (unaspirated vs. aspirated)
            \item some speakers have merged \emph{n-} into \emph{l-}, e.g. 你 \emph{nei5} / \emph{lei5} \enquote*{you (sg)} (vs. 李 \emph{lei5} surname)
            \item \emph{\textbf{j-}} is /j-/ (unusual for Cantonese Romanisations)
        \end{itemize}
    \end{column}

\section{Rimes}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{Codas}

\textbf{-p -m -t -n -k -ng -i -u}\\
/-p -m -t -n -k -ŋ -j -w/\\

\bigskip
\begin{itemize}
    \item /-p -t -k/: unreleased [p̚  t̚  k̚]
    \item highly stigmatised: errors with /-p -m/ 
    \item stigmatised: total or partial merger of /-k -ŋ/ into /-t -n/\\
    e.g. 香港 [hœŋ˥ kɔŋ˨˥] > [hœn˥ kɔn˨˥], 北角 [pɐk˥ kɔk˧] > [pɐt˥ kɔt˧]
    \item newer minor trends: \enquote{indeterminate} merger of /-n -ŋ/ as something like Ṽ, and /-t -k/ as something like ʔ\\
    e.g. 公安 [kʊŋ˥ ɔn˥] > [kʊ̃ ɔ̃], 失約 [sɐt˥ jœk˧] > [sɐʔ jœʔ]
\end{itemize}

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{Codas}

\textbf{-p -m -t -n -k -ng -i -u}\\
/-p -m -t -n -k -ŋ -j -w/\\

\bigskip
(Stereotypical) non-native accents, e.g.:
\begin{itemize}
    \item /-m/ > /-n/: e.g. Mandarin, Wu
    \item /-t -n/ > /-k -ŋ/: e.g. 潮州 Teochew
    \item /-p -m -t -n/ {\tiny partial or total} > /-k -ŋ/: \\莞寶粵 e.g. Gun2Bou2 Jyut6 / GuǎnBǎo Yuè 
    
\end{itemize}

\end{frame}
