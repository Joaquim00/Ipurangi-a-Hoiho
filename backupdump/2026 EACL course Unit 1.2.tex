\begin{frame}[fragile]{Cantonese}
De Sousa (2022), Cantonese:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Yuè dialect of Canton
    \item enclave Yuè varieties (\enquote{Cantonese enclaves}) resulted from the migration of people from the Canton area, primarily after the First Opium War (1840–1842)
    \item enclave Yuè varieties resulted from the migration of people from other Cantonese enclaves
\end{itemize}

\vfill{\tiny \setstretch{1} de Sousa, Hilário. 2022. The Expansion of Cantonese Over the Last Two Centuries. In Ye, Zhengdao (ed.), \emph{The Palgrave Handbook of Chinese Language Studies}, 409–440. Singapore: Springer Nature Singapore. \url{https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-16-0924-4_35}\\}
