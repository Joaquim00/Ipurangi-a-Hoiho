\begin{frame}[fragile]{Early Cantonese}
Earliest Cantonese rime book:\\ 
《分韻撮要》fan1 wan5 cyut3 jiu3 (early Qing dynasty)\\
\smallskip
{\footnotesize 
Earliest known version: 壁魚本《分韻撮要》\\eds. 武溪溫儀鳳岐山\& 姪武溪溫繼聖端石, undated (17xx ?)\\
\smallskip
Most-famous dated edition:\\
《江湖尺牘分韻撮要合集》(1782)}\\
\medskip
Only recorded literary words / pronunciations\\
\medskip
\textit{\small (Many older traits of Standard Cantonese can still be found in many other Sinitic varieties in the wider region)}

\end{frame}
